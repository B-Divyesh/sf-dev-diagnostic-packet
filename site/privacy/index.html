Privacy, in plain language.

Diagnostic Packet does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. The CLI has no telemetry and makes no network requests. Collection starts only when you run a command.

CLI data

Your manifest and captured evidence remain on your machine. Redaction happens before log evidence is written to the review folder. You decide whether and how to share the packet ZIP. Review it first: automatic redaction cannot recognize every project-specific secret.

Website data

The documentation site has no analytics, advertising, cookies, accounts, or third-party scripts. Its demo uses fixed sample data in separate browser storage. A service worker may cache public files for offline use. Clearing site data removes that cache.
