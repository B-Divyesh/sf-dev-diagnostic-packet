Public notice · 2026-08-27

Privacy, in plain language.

Diagnostic Packet does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. The CLI has no telemetry and makes no network requests. Collection starts only when you run a command.

CLI data

Your manifest and captured evidence remain on your machine. Redaction happens before log evidence is written to the review directory. You decide whether and how to share the exported ZIP. Review it first: automated redaction reduces risk but cannot guarantee that every project-specific secret is recognized.

Website data

The documentation site has no analytics, advertising, cookies, accounts, or third-party scripts. Its interactive demo uses fixed sample data and stores nothing. A service worker may cache public site files on your device so the documentation remains available offline; clearing site data removes that cache.

Contact

Report privacy or security concerns in the project’s public issue tracker. Do not include secrets or private packet contents in an issue.
